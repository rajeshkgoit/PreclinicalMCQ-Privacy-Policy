About PreclinicalMCQ

PreclinicalMCQ is a one-time-purchase MCQ app designed for medical students studying the basic medical sciences and preparing for exams.

What's Inside

  • 10,000+ practice questions across 7 subjects: Anatomy, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Pathology, Pharmacology, Physiology, and Community Medicine.
  • 100+ topics, organized from subject to topic to quiz.
  • Clear explanations for every question.
  • Flashcard Mode for every topic.
  • Performance Overview to check your progress.
  • Bookmarks to save important questions.
  • Mistake tracking to revisit questions you got wrong.
  • Exam Mode with clinical-vignette-style questions.
  • Beyond the books — medical stories and interesting facts worth knowing.
  • Daily study reminders to help you stay consistent with your preparation.

How It Works

Choose a subject, select a topic, and start practicing.

Answer one question at a time and get immediate feedback, including the correct answer and a concise explanation.

Prefer active recall? Switch to Flashcards and review every topic as a flip-card deck.

Your progress is automatically saved, so you can pick up right where you left off.

Why PreclinicalMCQ?

  • One-time purchase
  • No account required
  • No advertisements
  • No trackers or analytics

Whether you're reviewing individual topics, testing your knowledge, or preparing for exams, PreclinicalMCQ gives you a focused way to practice, review, and improve.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. PreclinicalMCQ does not require an account or registration.

Is PreclinicalMCQ a subscription?

No. PreclinicalMCQ is a one-time purchase. There is no recurring subscription.

Does the app contain advertisements?

No. PreclinicalMCQ does not contain advertisements.

Does the app use analytics or tracking?

No. PreclinicalMCQ does not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking services.

How is my study progress saved?

Your study progress is automatically saved on your device. Quiz progress, mistake history, bookmarks, and related study information are stored locally.

No account is required, and this information is not transmitted to us.

What subjects are included?

PreclinicalMCQ includes seven subjects: Anatomy, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Pathology, Pharmacology, Physiology, and Community Medicine.

How are the questions organized?

Questions are organized by subject and topic, allowing you to choose a specific area and practice questions one at a time.

How do I use Flashcards?

Select Flashcards, then choose a subject and topic to review questions and answers as a flip-card deck. Flashcards are designed to support active recall and topic-based review.

How do I use Exam Mode?

Exam Mode provides clinical-vignette-style questions so you can test your knowledge in an exam-oriented format.

How do I check my performance?

Use Performance Overview to review your study progress and see how you are performing across your practice.

How do bookmarks work?

You can bookmark important questions so you can easily return to them for later review.

How does mistake tracking work?

Questions you get wrong can be tracked so you can revisit them and focus on areas that need additional practice.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you previously purchased PreclinicalMCQ, make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

If you continue to have a problem accessing your purchase, please contact support using the email address below.

I found an incorrect question or answer. What should I do?

We welcome corrections and feedback. Please email us with the subject, topic, question, and a description of the issue. If possible, include a screenshot or reference that helps explain the correction.

I found a technical problem. What should I do?

Please email us and describe the problem. If possible, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, the app version, and a screenshot of the problem.

How do I contact support?
