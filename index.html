Privacy Policy for PreclinicalMCQ

Last updated: July 31, 2026

PreclinicalMCQ ("the app") is a study app for medical students, developed and maintained by Rajesh Goit ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

PreclinicalMCQ does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connections of any kind. Everything the app needs — every subject, topic, and question — is bundled inside the app itself and never leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information. It does not use cookies, device identifiers, or any tracking technology.

Quiz progress and scores

Your current quiz progress (which question you're on, your answers, and your score) is held only in the app's temporary memory while it's open. It is not saved to a file, not synced to any account, and not transmitted anywhere. Closing the app or restarting a quiz clears it.

Third-party services

PreclinicalMCQ does not integrate any third-party SDKs — no analytics platforms, no advertising networks, no crash reporting services, and no social media integrations. The app makes no network requests.

Children's privacy

PreclinicalMCQ is intended for medical students and other adult learners studying preclinical subjects. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because the app does not collect information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at rgoit@hotmail.com.
